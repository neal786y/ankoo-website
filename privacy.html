Privacy Policy

Ankoo – Maths for Kids · Effective 23 November 2026

Ankoo is a maths and problem-solving app for children aged 6–10, made by Nilesh Yadav, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, where it stays and what leaves your device. In short: we do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no servers that receive data from the app.

1. What the app stores on your device

To work, Ankoo keeps the following only on your phone or tablet:

  • Child profiles: an optional nickname (up to 12 characters), an Ankoo colour and a school level. No real name, age, birthday, photo, school or location is asked for.
  • Learning progress: levels, stars, answers and skill progress, coins, stickers and rewards, and daily play time.
  • Settings: daily time limit, sound, music and voice, reminder time, money and region (suggested from the country of your Google Play or App Store account, or else the device's region setting; never from your location), and whether share buttons are shown.
  • Subscription status: whether Ankoo Pro is active, as last confirmed by the app store.

We never receive this information. You can delete a child's profile or reset progress in the Grown-ups' Corner, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

2. Device backups

If backup is turned on for your device, Android (Google account backup) or iOS (iCloud or device backup) may include Ankoo's progress and settings, so they come back after you reinstall or move to a new phone. These backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; we cannot see them. The subscription status is never included in backups and is checked again with the store.

3. Purchases (Ankoo Pro)

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play (and, on iPhone and iPad, the App Store). We never see your payment details, name or email. The app asks the store on your device whether Ankoo Pro is active and keeps that answer on the device. The store's billing software included in the app may send technical information to Google (or Apple) to process and protect purchases; this is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (or Apple's Privacy Policy). Buying is only possible in the Grown-ups' Corner, behind a parental gate.

4. Sharing achievements

A grown-up can share a picture of a child's achievement, a weekly summary or an invitation, only from the Grown-ups' Corner. The picture is made on the device and leaves it only when the grown-up picks an app in the phone's share sheet. The child's nickname is added only if the grown-up turns "Show name" on for that share. Cards never show age, school, location, photos or areas where a child needs help. Shared messages contain a link to the app's store page with a tag naming the kind of share (achievement, weekly or invite), so the store can count installs from shares; the link contains nothing about you or your child. We never learn where a card was shared.

5. Reminders

Optional daily reminders and the weekly summary are scheduled on the device by the operating system. Nothing is sent to us, and no push notification service is used. You can turn them off in the Grown-ups' Corner or in the phone's settings.

6. Reporting a problem

"Report a problem" in the Grown-ups' Corner opens your own email app with a message to us containing question codes from the last activity, the content version, the app version and the operating system version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. We use it only to fix the problem and delete it on request.

7. Voice and speech

Ankoo reads questions aloud using recorded clips in the app or your device's own text-to-speech engine. Ankoo never uses the microphone or camera.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only if a grown-up turns reminders on.
  • Internet and billing: only so the app store can process and confirm subscriptions.

Ankoo does not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any device identifier.

9. Children's privacy

Ankoo is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families policy and the App Store Kids Category rules. Because we do not collect personal information from children or anyone else, there is nothing for us to share or sell. Links to stores and websites, purchases, sharing, ratings and permission prompts are all behind a parental gate. This approach is meant to meet the children's privacy rules of the places Ankoo is offered, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
